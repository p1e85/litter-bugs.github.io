1. What this is

Litter Troopers (“we,” “us,” operated by [P1 Creations LLC]) runs a reward program in certain sponsored areas of the map (“Sponsored Zones”). When you clean up litter inside a Sponsored Zone and follow the steps below, you may earn a cash reward, funded by that zone’s sponsor and paid out through Stripe.

By opting in to earn in a Sponsored Zone, you agree to these Payout Terms. If you don’t agree, don’t opt in — you can still use Litter Troopers normally.

2. This is a reward program, not employment

Participating does not make you an employee, contractor, agent, or partner of Litter Troopers or of any sponsor. There are no wages, no guaranteed hours, and no obligation on either side. You choose if and when to participate. Attorney: confirm this characterization holds in each operating state and adjust as needed.

3. How to earn

To be eligible for a reward on a route, all of the following must be true:

  1. You opted in to earning when prompted at the start of the route.
  2. Your route started and ended inside the same Sponsored Zone.
  3. You photographed each spot you cleaned — a “before” photo when you tag it and an “after” photo once it’s clean.
  4. At the end, you took a haul photo of what you collected and entered your honest estimate of how many full bags you filled.

Standard, non-sponsored cleanups never require any of this and never earn a reward.

4. How rewards are calculated

Rewards are based on full standard kitchen-size (about 13-gallon) trash bags:

  • $5 for each full bag.
  • A $10 bonus for every 5 full bags.
  • An admin may add a discretionary bonus — for example, if you used larger contractor bags that hold more than a standard bag.

Rates are set by Litter Troopers and may change. The rate in effect is the one applied at the time your claim is approved.

5. Every reward is reviewed and approved by us

Your bag count is an estimate. Before any reward is granted, a Litter Troopers admin reviews your photos and may:

  • Adjust the bag count up or down to match what the photos show;
  • Approve the claim at the adjusted amount; or
  • Reject the claim.

Following the steps does not guarantee payment. A reward is only earned once an admin approves it. Submitting inaccurate, staged, duplicated, or misleading claims may result in rejection, forfeiture of your balance, and removal from the program.

6. Rewards are limited to the zone’s available funds

Each Sponsored Zone has a limited pool of sponsor-provided funds. Rewards are paid from that pool. If a zone’s remaining funds are less than your calculated reward, you will receive only what remains in the pool, and the zone may stop offering rewards until it is refunded. We may pause, close, or remove a Sponsored Zone at any time.

7. Your balance and cashing out

  • Approved rewards accrue to your Litter Troopers payout balance. Your balance can be built up across any Sponsored Zone you clean.
  • You can cash out once your balance reaches $100 (USD).
  • Balances are not cash, have no value outside this program, cannot be transferred to another person, and may be forfeited if your account is removed for violating these terms or our general Terms of Service.

8. Payments are made through Stripe — a Stripe account is required

All cash-outs are processed by Stripe through Stripe Connect. To receive money, you must:

  • Create and complete onboarding for a Stripe Connect account, including any identity and bank information Stripe requires; and
  • Accept Stripe’s terms in addition to these terms.

Stripe — not Litter Troopers — holds your payment and banking relationship, verifies your identity, and moves the funds. If you don’t set up or maintain a valid Stripe account, we can’t pay you, and rewards can’t be cashed out. We are not responsible for delays, holds, or decisions made by Stripe.

9. Withdrawal fee

A withdrawal fee applies to each cash-out, and you pay it — it is deducted from the amount you withdraw, so the amount that reaches your bank may be less than your balance. We’ll show the fee before you confirm a cash-out.

10. Taxes

Any rewards you receive may be taxable income, and you are solely responsible for any taxes on them. Litter Troopers does not withhold taxes. Depending on the amount and your location, tax forms may be issued by us or by Stripe under applicable rules and their tax-reporting features. 11. Your photos and information

Photos and information you submit for a reward (before/after photos, haul photos, bag counts, location, and your username) may be reviewed by our admins and used to verify and administer the program. Aggregate cleanup activity and your username may be shown to the zone’s sponsor and on the community map, consistent with our Privacy Policy. Don’t submit photos containing other people, license plates, or private information you don’t have the right to share.

We may change these terms, the reward rates, the cash-out threshold, or the program itself at any time, and we may suspend or end the program or any Sponsored Zone. Changes apply going forward. Continuing to participate after a change means you accept it.
