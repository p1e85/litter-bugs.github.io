Get started in minutes

Three steps to your first cleanup.

No downloads required on web. Open the app, allow location, and go. It really is that simple.

Step 01
📍
Open the app & start tracking

Hit Start Tracking and your GPS route begins. Walk, pick up litter, and the app records every step. No sign-up needed to try it.

Step 02
📸
Pin what you find

Tap the camera button to photograph and geotag litter as you go. Each pin adds to the global map showing where the problem areas are.

Step 03
🚀
Publish & earn your XP

Finish your cleanup and publish your route to the community map. Earn XP based on distance and pins, level up, and climb the leaderboard.

Step 04
🛡️
Join or build a squad

Create or join a local squad to combine stats, compete as a team, and take on squad quests together. Cleanups are better with a crew.

The XP & Level System

Every cleanup moves you forward.

XP is earned for routes, pins, events, and squad milestones. A daily curve keeps things fair — the more you do, the more you earn, just at a sustainable rate.
