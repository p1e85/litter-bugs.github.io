About Litter Bugs

The Mission (Why)

Litter Bugs transforms the chore of cleaning up our neighborhoods into a visible and rewarding achievement. By tracking your cleanup routes and pinning the litter you collect, you create a tangible record of your positive impact.

How to Use the App

  1. Start Tracking: Open the menu and press 'Start Tracking' to draw your path on the map.
  2. Pin Litter: When you find litter, press the 'Pin Picture' camera button to take a photo.
  3. Manage Data: Use the 'Data Management' button to save, load, or export your current session.
  4. Publish: When logged in, 'Publish to Community' shares your completed route.
  5. Community View: Toggle the 'Community View' button to see all routes published by other users!
