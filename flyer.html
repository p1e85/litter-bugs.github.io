TIRED OF TRASH?

Clean Up Our Parks. Get Rewarded.

Litter Troopers is a new, free app that turns your cleanup walk into a game. Track your impact, earn achievements, and join a community dedicated to making our world cleaner.

1. TRACK YOUR ROUTE

Use your phone's GPS to map your cleanup path. See the ground you've covered and the difference you've made.

2. PIN THE LITTER

Find some trash? Snap a photo to geotag its location on the map. Every pin is a victory for our community.

3. EARN REWARDS

Unlock achievement badges, climb the community leaderboard, and organize cleanup meetups with your neighbors.
