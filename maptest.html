The Mission (Why)

How to Use the App

  1. Start Tracking: Open the menu and press 'Start Tracking'...
  2. Pin Litter: When you find litter, press the 'Pin Picture' camera button...
  3. Manage Data: Use the 'Data Management' button to save, load, or export...
  4. Publish: When logged in, 'Publish to Community' shares your completed route...
  5. Community View: Toggle the 'Community View' button to see all routes...
